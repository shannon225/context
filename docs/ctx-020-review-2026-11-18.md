## Checked and found OK

- **TDC losers.** They are never forced again or given a second chance.
- **E-value null.** It is still computed from all scans in the run.
- **Background-only behavior.** It is unchanged apart from the approved CTX020-035 range-advance fix.
- **Detection status.**
  - Precedence: NO_REFERENCE > NOT_DETECTED > DETECTED.
  - DETECTED is decided by the target alone.
  - TDC_LOSER is derived after `trim(1)`.
- **Ambiguous references.** These throw.
- **Error handling.** I/O and SQL errors are wrapped with their cause. Non-finite features throw.
- **Report writing.** The report is atomic and written only after success. Unknown outputs are refused.
- **Cache identity.** It is versioned (`assay-full-gradient-scoring-v2`).
- **Schema.** There are no new numeric columns, and the pairing schema is unchanged.
- **Concurrency.** The tracker is synchronized.
